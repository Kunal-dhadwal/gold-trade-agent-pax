# GOLD AI TRADING AGENT — PROJECT CONSTITUTION
*Strict Engineering Governance, Quantitative Rigor, Risk-First Rules, and Architectural Invariants*

---

## 1. MISSION & FOUNDATIONAL PURPOSE

The Gold AI Trading Agent is an institutional-grade, multi-agent quantitative research and execution system dedicated exclusively to **Gold (XAU/USD and Delta Exchange Gold derivatives)**. 

The objective of this system is **NOT** to claim or guarantee profits.
The objective is:
> **Discover, validate, and execute trading strategies with statistically robust positive expectancy, strictly controlled drawdowns, high repeatability, and verifiable out-of-sample resilience.**

---

## 2. CORE CONSTITUTIONAL TENETS

### Tenet I: The Four-Phase Sequenced Boundary
Development must adhere strictly to the 4 sequential phases:
1. **Phase 1: Foundation, Data Ingestion & Quality Engine, and Deterministic Backtester**
2. **Phase 2: AI Strategy Factory, Multi-Agent Architecture, and Multi-Level Overfitting Validation**
3. **Phase 3: Real-Time Market Feed, Paper Trading Engine, Deterministic Risk Guardrails & React Dashboard**
4. **Phase 4: Delta Exchange Production Execution, Reconciliation, VPS Deployment & Live Safety Protocols**

*Invariant:* No code from Phase $N+1$ may be merged or activated before Phase $N$ has achieved 100% test passing, security audit, code review, documentation sign-off, and formal completion reporting.

---

### Tenet II: Deterministic Risk Precedence & Separation of AI
1. **AI is never the executioner:**
   $$\text{Market Data} \longrightarrow \text{AI / Strategy Signal} \longrightarrow \mathbf{Deterministic\ Risk\ Engine} \longrightarrow \mathbf{Execution\ Engine} \longrightarrow \text{Exchange}$$
2. The Risk Engine is strictly deterministic, written in pure Python/C-level logic, and holds absolute veto authority over any signal produced by an AI model, human operator, or strategy heuristic.
3. If any risk parameter is breached (drawdown, max daily loss, exposure, spread widening, abnormal market volatility, missing SL), the trade is cancelled immediately: **NO EXCEPTIONS**.

---

### Tenet III: Separation of Computational Concerns (LLM vs. Deterministic Quant)
- **Deterministic Python / NumPy / SciPy / Rust / Pandas Engine:**
  - Indicator computation
  - Bar-by-bar backtesting
  - Real-time order book processing and candle construction
  - Position sizing, equity curve tracking, risk evaluations
  - Monte Carlo permutations and Walk-Forward optimization
  - Slippage and fee modeling
- **AI / LLM Agents (Claude / Gemini / GPT):**
  - Market regime reasoning and narrative synthesis
  - Hypothesis generation and strategy specification drafting
  - Parameter proposals within mathematically sound boundaries
  - Post-mortem trade analysis and failure mode diagnosis
  - Pine Script v5 transpilation from validated mathematical rules
  - Research report summarization

*Invariant:* Under no circumstance shall raw candle tick streams be streamed directly into LLM prompts. LLMs operate strictly on structured mathematical and statistical feature summaries.

---

### Tenet IV: Absolute Data Quality and Historical Integrity
1. **Raw data is immutable.** Raw payloads from Yahoo Finance, Delta Exchange, or external tick archives must be permanently archived without modification in `data/raw/`.
2. **Normalization must be reproducible.** Normalized datasets in `data/normalized/` or `data/timeframe/` must be generated by idempotent deterministic pipelines.
3. Every candle batch must be validated against:
   - $High \ge \max(Open, Close)$
   - $Low \le \min(Open, Close)$
   - $Volume \ge 0$
   - Monotonic UTC timestamp ordering ($t_{i} > t_{i-1}$)
   - Detection of duplicate bars, gaps, and anomalous price spikes ($> 5\sigma$ price shock filter)
4. Corrupted data must never be silently smoothed or filled without explicit, auditable flags.

---

### Tenet V: Anti-Overfitting & Out-of-Sample Sanctity
1. **Strict Data Segregation:**
   - **In-Sample (Training / Exploration):** 60%
   - **Validation (Parameter Selection):** 20%
   - **Out-of-Sample (OOS Test Gate):** 20%
2. The Out-of-Sample partition must **never** be accessed during strategy parameter tuning or exploratory research.
3. Every candidate strategy must survive:
   - Walk-Forward Analysis (WFA) with walk-forward efficiency $> 0.60$
   - Monte Carlo trade reshuffling ($p(\text{ruin}) < 0.01$, 95% worst-case DD within risk budget)
   - Parameter sensitivity stability (no fragile knife-edge optima)
   - Minimum trade sample criteria ($N \ge 100$ trades)
4. A strategy that shows stellar in-sample metrics but degrades sharply out-of-sample is classified as **OVERFITTED** and permanently rejected or retired.

---

### Tenet VI: Operational Safety and Kill Switches
1. **Live Trading Disabled By Default:** `LIVE_TRADING_ENABLED=false` must remain hardcoded across all configuration templates until every validation gate (Backtest, OOS, Walk-Forward, Monte Carlo, Paper Trading 30-day trial) is certified.
2. **Deterministic Kill Switch:** Automatically halts trading, cancels all pending orders, and optionally flattens positions if:
   - Daily loss limit is hit (e.g., $2.0\%$ of account equity).
   - Maximum portfolio drawdown threshold is breached (e.g., $6.0\%$).
   - Data feed gap $> 3$ consecutive heartbeat intervals.
   - Any order lacks a verified Stop Loss.
   - Exchange API latency $> 2000$ ms or returns consecutive rate-limit/network errors.

---

### Tenet VII: Honest Terminology and Evidence Classification
Every strategy must carry an explicit lifecycle status:
- `RESEARCH`: Conceptual hypothesis stage.
- `BACKTESTED`: Preliminary backtest completed on in-sample data.
- `VALIDATION`: Undergoing Walk-Forward, Monte Carlo, and stress tests.
- `OOS_PASSED`: Certified on out-of-sample holdout data.
- `PAPER_TRADING`: Active in real-time simulated execution.
- `LIVE_CANDIDATE`: Passed minimum paper trading duration with confirmed execution realism.
- `ACTIVE`: Executing real capital on Delta Exchange under strict risk caps.
- `REJECTED`: Failed mathematical, validation, or risk criteria.
- `RETIRED`: Degraded in live/paper trading beyond acceptable degradation thresholds.

---

### Tenet VIII: Free-First Architectural Efficiency
1. No paid APIs, proprietary databases, or fee-based data subscriptions are required for operation.
2. The platform operates on standard developer hardware (laptop or modest Linux VPS) utilizing Parquet column storage, PostgreSQL, Redis, FastAPI, and Vite/React.
3. Compute efficiency is paramount: batch processing, zero-copy PyArrow operations, and vectorization must be preferred over naive Python loops.
